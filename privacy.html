Privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

  • This site has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Your payment goes through Ziina and your booking through Calendly. Noha receives what you give them so she can hold your call.
  • Your details are used only for your call and the records the law requires. They are never sold.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Noha Hefny, trading as The World Taught Me (business name and licence details to be confirmed). Questions about your data: Noha’s contact email (to be added).

What this site itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no contact form and no analytics. Your browser remembers two small things in local storage: the language you chose (English or Arabic), and that you opened the payment step, so the booking card can show you the next step when you come back. Both stay on your device and you can clear them by clearing your browser’s site data.

The Sunday letter

If you sign up for the Sunday letter, you finish signing up on the newsletter provider’s page (provider to be confirmed, e.g. Substack), which handles your email address under its own privacy policy. You can unsubscribe from any letter with one click.

When you pay with Ziina

The "Pay with Ziina" button opens Ziina’s own checkout page. Ziina processes your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay payment under its own privacy policy; your card details never pass through this site or reach Noha. Noha sees the payment record Ziina shows her, such as your name, the amount, the date and any note you add, so she can match your payment to your booking.

When you book with Calendly

The calendar is provided by Calendly and only loads when you choose to pick a time. Calendly collects the details you type in (usually your name, email address and any answers you give) under its own privacy policy and shares them with Noha so the call can happen. Calendly sends your confirmation and reminder emails.

When you email

If you email Noha (for example a speaking or media inquiry), she receives your email address and whatever you write, and uses it only to reply.

Other services that load with the page

Like any website, a few providers see your IP address and basic browser information when they deliver parts of the page: the web host, Google Fonts (typefaces, including the Arabic ones), cdnjs by Cloudflare and jsDelivr (small animation and smooth-scrolling scripts). They receive this only to deliver those files. Photos on the site are stored on the site itself.

Why your details are used and how long they are kept

  • To hold your call, confirm your payment and follow up about the call you booked.
  • To keep financial records for as long as UAE law requires.
  • Booking details not needed for records are deleted when they are no longer needed for your call, and within 12 months at the latest.

Noha does not sell your details, add you to a mailing list without asking, or use them for advertising.

Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you can ask to see the personal data Noha holds about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted (unless the law requires it to be kept), and object to how it is used. Email Noha’s contact email (to be added) and you will get a reply within 30 days.

Under 18

Calls are for adults. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian needs to book and pay for you.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
