Terms & bookings

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

  • Pay with Ziina first, then pick your time on Calendly.
  • Need a different time? Reschedule up to 24 hours before the call.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before for a full refund. Later cancellations and no-shows are not refunded.
  • If Noha has to cancel, you choose a new time or a full refund.

Who you are booking with

Calls are with Noha Hefny, trading as The World Taught Me (business name, licence and emirate to be confirmed). Contact: Noha's contact email (to be added).

How booking works

  1. You pay the price shown on the site and on Ziina's checkout page, in UAE dirhams (AED).
  2. You come back to the site and choose a time in Calendly. Please use the same name you paid with.
  3. Calendly emails you the confirmation and call details.

Your booking is confirmed once the payment and the Calendly booking can be matched. A booking made without a matching payment may be cancelled. If you paid but could not book a time, email Noha's contact email (to be added) and Noha will sort it out with you.

Rescheduling and cancelling

  • You can reschedule once, using the link in your Calendly email, up to 24 hours before the call.
  • If you cancel at least 24 hours before the call, you get a full refund.
  • If you cancel later than that, or do not join the call, the fee is not refunded.
  • If you join late, the call still ends at the booked time.
  • If Noha needs to cancel or move the call, you can choose a new time or a full refund.

Refunds go back through Ziina to the way you paid. How long the money takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

What a call is, and is not

A 1:1 call is a conversation in which Noha shares her experience and perspective as a thought partner. It is not legal, financial, medical, psychological or other professional advice, and it does not create an employment or consulting relationship. The decisions you make afterwards are your own.

Calls are not recorded unless you and Noha both agree first. What you share is kept private.

Lessons and content on this site

The lessons, manifesto and other writing on this site belong to Noha Hefny. You are welcome to share links and short quotes with credit. Please ask before republishing anything in full.

Payments and third parties

Payments are processed by Ziina and bookings are handled by Calendly, each under its own terms. Noha is not responsible for outages or errors on their side, but will help you sort out any problem with a booking you paid for.

Liability

As far as the law allows, Noha's total responsibility to you for a call is limited to the amount you paid for it. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under UAE consumer protection law (Federal Law No. 15 of 2020).

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the emirate where the business is licensed, and any dispute goes to the courts there.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The version posted here when you pay is the one that applies to your call.
