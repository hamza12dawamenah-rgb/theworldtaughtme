About Noha

Communication changes lives, because it changes people.

My work has taken me from humanitarian operations with the United Nations to multinational organizations and social entrepreneurship. Three chapters taught me the most.

  1. 01

    The United Nations

    What the world’s hardest problems taught me about leadership.

    Leadership under pressure is not about having answers. It is about holding space for others to find them. Trust is built in the margins, not the meetings.

  2. 02

    Multinational organizations

    What business taught me about purpose.

    The most successful organizations are not those with the best strategies. They are those with the clearest sense of why they exist.

  3. 03

    Social entrepreneurship

    Why purpose requires courage.

    Purpose without courage is just intention. The leaders who create lasting change act before they are ready.
